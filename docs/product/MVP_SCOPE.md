## Recherche bornée

Combiner les filtres avec ET entre familles et OU entre valeurs d’une famille. Tri par nom ou dernière mise à jour éditoriale ; ordre stable avec critère secondaire. Montrer le nombre de résultats, l’état vide et la réinitialisation. La recherche n’indexe que les contenus publiés. La traduction des requêtes, la recherche sémantique et les tolérances avancées sont reportées.

## Pages nécessaires

Accueil ; catalogue et filtres ; fiche historique ; fiche renaissance ; connexion et profil privé minimal ; proposer/corriger ; suivi des contributions ; revue de modération ; pages explicatives et liens de signalement. Ces besoins cadrent la future UX, sans imposer de maquette ou navigation définitive.

## Arbitrage de charge

La revue avant publication limite le vandalisme mais concentre le travail sur le fondateur. En cas de surcharge : limiter temporairement les soumissions avec un message explicite, sans publication automatique ni délégation implicite. Une version catalogue éditorial seul est le repli recommandé si F05–F07 dépassent les moyens disponibles.

## English

# Proposed MVP scope

The five recommendations and priorities below were approved on 19 October 2026 (RF-007/RF-008). P0 means required for the recommended MVP; P1 means after launch; out of MVP means excluded from initial development.

| ID | Priority | Feature and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F01 | P0 | Editorial homepage: mission, discovery, status explanations and contribution invitation |
| F02 | P0 | Paginated catalogue; name, alias and summary search; category, technology, known decade, rights and historical-status filters |
| F03 | P0 | Historical record with sources, creators, timeline, rights, last verification and related revivals |
| F04 | P0 | Lightweight revival: objective, relationship to original, public maintainers, status, external links and verification date |
| F05 | P0 | GitHub contributor sign-in, minimal profile and sign-out; no local passwords |
| F06 | P0 | Project, revival and correction submissions; personal outcome tracking; no uploads |
| F07 | P0 | Moderation queue, review, reasons and publication history; founder only initially |
| F08 | P0 | Issue links for reports and removal requests under RF-004; no confidentiality promise |
| F09 | P0 | FR/EN interface and launch editorial content; explicit contribution language and missing translations |
| F10 | P1 | Bookmarks, notifications, richer public profiles and editorial translation of every contribution |
| F11 | Out of MVP | Comments, forum, messaging, votes, reputation and self-managed teams |
| F12 | Out of MVP | Code hosting, releases, downloadable files, bulk scraping/import, automated license analysis, payments and mobile app |

## Bounded search

Combine filter families with AND and values within a family with OR. Sort by name or editorial update date, with stable secondary ordering. Show result counts, empty states and reset controls. Search only published content. Query translation, semantic search and advanced fuzzy matching are deferred.

## Required pages

Home; catalogue and filters; historical record; revival page; sign-in and minimal private profile; submission/correction; contribution tracking; moderation review; explanatory pages and reporting links. These needs inform future UX without prescribing mockups or final navigation.

## Workload tradeoff

Review before publication reduces vandalism but concentrates work on the founder. Under excessive load, temporarily limit submissions with an explicit message, without automatic publishing or implicit delegation. An editorial-only catalogue is the recommended fallback if F05–F07 exceed available capacity.
