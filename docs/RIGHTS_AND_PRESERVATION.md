# Rights and responsible preservation

**Review status:** proposed operational guidance. It does not constitute legal advice or grant rights over third-party material.

## Separate the activities

Assess historical referencing, metadata preservation, links to external archives, code reuse, file distribution, revival development, and trademark or media use separately. Permission for one activity does not establish permission for another.

Public source code is not automatically licensed for reuse. Age or inactivity does not establish legal abandonment. An unknown license means rights remain unverified.

## Source records

For each future historical record, capture original name, creators, known dates, source URL, access date, evidence supporting claims, license text or reference, rights-holder information where appropriate, and uncertainty.

Separate facts, community recollections, and hypotheses. Credit original authors and distinguish independent revivals from official continuations. Screenshots, logos, archives, and source files require their own rights assessment.

## Proposed review process

1. Receive a sourced proposal containing only material the contributor is entitled to submit.
2. Verify provenance and identify the rights applicable to each planned use.
3. Record unknowns and restrictions without inferring permission.
4. Request founder approval for sensitive publication or revival decisions.
5. Preserve source and correction history while protecting personal data.

A project can be documented without being eligible for revival. Automated license detection assists review but cannot settle ambiguous ownership or permissions.

## Corrections and removal requests

The founder selected [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rebornforge/.github/issues/new/choose) for corrections, reports, and removal requests. Identify the affected URL, requested action, and public supporting references. Do not attach identity documents, private ownership evidence, or personal data. No confidential channel is currently designated.

The proposed process is human-led review, identification of affected content, proportionate temporary restrictions where authorized, verification of evidence, a recorded decision, and communication through the issue. Applicable legal deadlines require assessment when the service's operating jurisdiction is established.

## Xnova Reforged

No official listing, transfer, code change, or reuse of Xnova Reforged content is authorized by this documentation phase. Confirm rights and obtain explicit founder approval before official referencing.
