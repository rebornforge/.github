## English

# RF-011 — Visual direction selection

- **Status:** Approved.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Subject:** Homepage identity.
- **Context:** [Three viewable boards](../design/phase-3/README.md), FR/EN copy and limitations.
- **AI proposal:** A Digital Forge, B Digital Heritage, C Modern Community.
- **Alternatives:** Select, specify a combination, request revisions or reject all.
- **Recommendation:** B's heritage identity with C's simple controls; reduce decoration and omit public-looking avatars.
- **Founder decision:** Explicit selection of C — Modern Community on 19 October 2026.
- **Consequences:** C is the reference direction: bright layout, blue accents and approachable community simplicity. Refine contrast, small text and mobile; replace decorative avatars implying out-of-MVP public profiles. Exact tokens, fonts and logo remain to be formalized. Selection does not approve every image detail or authorize merge/phase 4; request its launch before detailed mockups. No prototype or final code.
