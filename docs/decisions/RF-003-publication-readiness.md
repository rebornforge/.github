## Français

# RF-003 — Publication des fondations

- **Statut :** Proposed.
- **Date :** 2026-10-12 ; actualisation linguistique le 2026-10-13.
- **Sujet :** décisions avant fusion et ouverture communautaire.
- **Contexte :** documentation autorisée ; identité technique, licences et publication restent à préciser.
- **Proposition IA :** examiner la PR, valider la présentation bilingue, vérifier le compte du fondateur et définir les licences.
- **Alternatives :** demander des modifications ou différer l’ouverture.
- **Recommandation :** conserver un brouillon. Étudier CC BY 4.0 pour les textes originaux, ou CC BY-SA 4.0 pour la réciprocité ; traiter les modèles séparément, exclure contenus tiers et marques, choisir séparément la licence du code.
- **Décision :** publication et licences en attente. DiDroD et issues approuvés par RF-004 ; français prioritaire et traduction anglaise approuvés par RF-005.
- **Conséquences :** aucun LICENSE ni CODEOWNERS actif. Vérifier que le fondateur peut approuver des PR créées par l’identité de contribution. Évaluer les limites des signalements publics avant lancement applicatif.

## English

# RF-003 — Foundation publication readiness

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Subject:** Decisions required before foundation merge and community launch.
- **Context:** Documentation is authorized, but specific identities, contacts, licensing and publication decisions remain unresolved.
- **AI proposal:** Review the foundation PR, confirm the bilingual public presentation, verify the founder's GitHub login and permissions, and select licensing terms for original documentation.
- **Alternatives:** Request revisions; defer broader community participation until policy and contacts are ready.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the PR in draft until publication choices are resolved. Consider CC BY 4.0 for original prose or CC BY-SA 4.0 if reciprocal sharing of adaptations is preferred. Assess templates separately; exclude third-party works and trademarks. Decide code licensing separately.
- **Founder decision:** Publication and licensing pending. The founder identifies as DiDroD and selected issues for reports and removal requests; see RF-004. French-first publication with English translations is approved by RF-005.
- **Consequences:** No LICENSE or active CODEOWNERS file is added now. Do not activate mandatory review without checking that the founder can approve PRs authored through the selected contributor identity. Public reporting limits and future confidential reporting needs remain to be assessed before application launch.
