# Governance

## Founding authority

The founder is RebornForge's sole strategic, functional, and organizational decision-maker. The founding instructions establish this authority; documentation preparation does not delegate it.

The founder decides vision, priorities, scope, architecture, technologies, design, licenses, costs, hosting, community governance, sensitive permissions, integrations, publications, deployments, and pull-request merges. Silence never constitutes permission.

The founder identifies as DiDroD. The connected GitHub account is enamaryn; whether DiDroD is the account login or display name, and its repository permissions, have not been verified. No CODEOWNERS assignment is added until a valid account with write access is confirmed.

## AI and contributor responsibilities

AI agents analyze, recommend, implement authorized work, test, document, and prepare pull requests. Contributors propose improvements within published contribution rules. Neither consensus nor technical coordination replaces founder approval.

Multiple agents may specialize when actually available and appropriate. Collaboration must never be invented. The technical coordinator consolidates findings and records unresolved decisions.

Routine reversible implementation details may be handled autonomously only when they respect approved scope, incur no cost, do not change sensitive permissions or structural security, and do not affect other projects.

## Approval gates

Each roadmap phase requires explicit approval before moving to the next. Preparation of a prototype is not application-development authorization.

- Three genuinely distinct homepage concepts precede selection of a visual direction.
- Detailed desktop/mobile mockups require approval before interactive prototyping.
- The prototype technology requires approval before execution.
- Final application development requires explicit approval of mockups and the interactive prototype.
- Merging any pull request and deploying any release require founder authorization.

No direct push to main, automatic merge, unauthorized paid service, secret disclosure, protection bypass, repository transfer, or repository deletion is permitted.

## Decision process

A substantial proposal states objective, recommendation, alternatives, justification, risks, and the decision requested. Record the founder's response and consequences in the [decision register](docs/decisions/README.md).

Only explicit decisions receive Approved status. Record changes to earlier decisions as Superseded without deleting history. Flag conflicts and follow the latest applicable approved decision.

## Community governance

For the founding stage, moderation remains human-led under founder authority. Moderator roles, enforcement permissions, appeals, and private reporting channels require a separately approved policy before operation.
