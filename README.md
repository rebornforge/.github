## Status and scope

The founder authorized preparation of foundation documentation on 12 October 2026. Approval to prepare documents does not approve every proposed policy, license, public description, or security setting. This package remains subject to founder review and explicit merge authorization.

Shared community files are provided from this public repository. Repository-specific files can override supported defaults. Licenses, CODEOWNERS, and workflows must be handled per repository; they are not automatically inherited.

No license has been selected. Public visibility does not grant a general reuse or redistribution license. Original creators retain their rights, and third-party material remains subject to its own terms.
